About

What is Weavo

Weavo is a collaborative art project. Anyone can submit a piece of art and watch it become part of something larger — every submission is placed into a shared weavo based on its dominant color, building a picture that only comes together because everyone contributed a piece.

Why we built this

The project centers neurodivergent and neurotypical artists working side by side, with no separation between the two. Every piece counts equally toward the whole, regardless of who made it or how.

How it works

  1. Pick a project and upload a piece of art.
  2. Weavo reads its dominant color automatically — no tagging required.
  3. It's slotted into an open cell in the shared grid, wherever that color fits best. Nobody places pieces by hand.
  4. The picture builds itself one submission at a time, and only resolves into a whole once enough pieces have landed.

It's a mosaic in reverse: a mosaicist starts with a picture in mind and cuts pieces to fit it. Weavo starts with nothing but color logic — the artwork comes first, from people who've never seen each other's work, and the image only emerges in hindsight. Step back and it reads like any mosaic. Step closer and each "tile" turns out to be someone's complete artwork.

Who's behind it

Weavo is an independent, community-built project. Questions, feedback, or ideas — reach out at jadyenthelee@gmail.com.

Privacy Policy

Last updated July 2026

What we collect

When you sign in with Google, we receive your name, email, and profile photo through our authentication provider, Supabase. If you fill out your profile, we also store what you add: username, bio, links, and country/city. Anything you submit — artwork, comments, likes, saves — is stored too.

How we use it

To create and display your public profile and submitted artwork, and to power community features like likes, comments, saved artwork, and artist stats. We don't sell your data or use it for advertising.

Where it's stored

Data and images are stored with Supabase (hosted database and file storage). Sign-in is handled by Supabase Auth via Google, and we never see or store your Google password.

What's public

Weavo is a public community gallery, not a private space. Your profile (name, avatar, username, bio, tags, country/city, links), submitted artwork, and comments are visible to anyone who visits the site.

Your choices

  • Edit or remove your profile info anytime from your profile page.
  • Delete comments or artwork you've posted.
  • Email us to request full account deletion — we'll remove your account and associated data.

Cookies & sessions

We use local storage/cookies only to keep you signed in. There are no third-party advertising or tracking cookies.

Children

Weavo isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect data from them.

Questions or deletion requests: jadyenthelee@gmail.com
