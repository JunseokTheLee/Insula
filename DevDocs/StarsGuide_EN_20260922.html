✦ Weavo Pieces of People, A Brighter Sky  ·  Constellations guide

A night sky the games leave behind

Every time you finish an artwork in a Weavo game, one star lights up in your sky. Star by star, thirteen constellations fill in, from Aries to Scorpius.

1. How a star lights up

Finish an artwork in Find the Piece or Color by Number and that artwork becomes a star. The two games are separate stars, so finishing the same artwork in both gives you two.

  • Playing the same artwork again does not add a star.
  • Finishing a harder Color by Number board makes the star you already have burn bigger.
  • Find the Piece stars are warm gold; Color by Number stars are blue.

2. Thirteen constellations

Constellations fill in order, smallest first: from Aries (4 stars) through Delphinus, Cancer, Capricornus, Phoenix, Leo, Cygnus, Pavo, Lepus, Taurus, Pegasus and Pisces to Scorpius (17 stars) — 121 stars in all. Fill all thirteen and a second sky begins, in the same order.

Each constellation is an animal drawn in light. Finished ones shine, the one being filled shows where its next star goes, and the ones still ahead wait as faint shapes.

3. Where to find it

🎮The "My constellations" card in the game menu
👤The constellation strip on your profile
✦The note on the result screen after a game

The address is weavo.art/en/stars. Drag the sky sideways to look around, or use “View large” to fill the screen. Tap a star to see its artwork card, then tap the card to open it. The sky goes round without end, and any finished constellation can be shared as a picture.

Common questions

If an artwork is removed, do I lose its star?
No. A star keeps the artwork title and the artist's name with it, so it stays in your sky even if the picture comes down.
Is there a ranking?
No. Constellations are for collecting, not competing. There is no number that compares you with anyone.
Can I keep mine private?
Yes — turn on "Hide my constellations from others" on the constellations page and nobody else can open your sky.
Do I need an account?
You can play without one, but stars are only kept for a signed-in account.
Do games I played earlier count?
Yes. Everything finished before the feature opened was carried over into stars.
