About

What is Weavo

Weavo is a collaborative art platform. Anyone can upload a piece of art, and it becomes one piece of something larger. Every artwork counts equally toward the whole, regardless of who made it or how — a space where artists with and without disabilities are connected side by side through their work, with no separation between the two.

What you can do

01Weavo Campaign

A mosaic we finish together

Anyone can submit an artwork, and every submission is placed into one large mosaic image based on its dominant color.

From a distance it reads as a single finished picture; up close, every cell is a different artist's work. It's a collaborative art campaign where many people's pieces come together into one shared artwork.

  • No campaign to pick — the color is read automatically and finds the spot it fits best.
  • Tap any cell to see the whole original artwork and its artist.
  • Every artwork carries its stories — the story behind the piece (About the artwork) and how the artist works (About the process).

03Portfolio

Your own exhibition

Pick any of your uploaded artworks and arrange them into a portfolio of your own.

  • Set the order and the cover artwork yourself.
  • Choose a grid, masonry or story layout, or play it as a slideshow.
  • Choose who sees it: public, link-only (anyone with the link) or private.
  • Share the link and a preview card with its title and cover goes along.

How a mosaic comes together

Every upload's dominant color is read automatically and matched to the best open cell across every active campaign — so the full picture comes into focus as more artists add their work.

Mosaic

From a single upload to a place in the whole.

  1. 01

    Upload your artwork

    Upload any medium from your profile. Along with a title, you can add About the artwork (the story behind the piece) and About the process (how you work — your methods, tools, surroundings). Your process story is pre-filled from last time on your next upload. There's no campaign to pick.

  2. 02

    It is cut into pieces and its colors read

    Your artwork is cut into small pieces, and each piece's dominant color is analyzed automatically. No tagging required.

  3. 03

    Each piece finds its spot

    Every piece is placed into whichever open cell, across every active campaign, its color fits best. Nobody places pieces by hand or chooses which campaign they join.

  4. 04

    The picture comes together

    The image builds itself one piece at a time and only resolves into a whole once enough have landed. Tap any cell to see the original artwork, its artist, and its stories.

It's a mosaic in reverse: a mosaicist starts with a picture in mind and cuts pieces to fit it. Weavo starts with nothing but color logic — the artwork comes first, from people who've never seen each other's work, and the image only emerges in hindsight. Step back and it reads like any mosaic. Step closer and each "tile" turns out to be someone's complete artwork.

Who's behind it

Weavo is an initiative by ABST, a Jeju Island-based organization that brings together high school art students and local autistic/disabled youth artists to build exhibitions rooted in real collaboration. Questions, feedback, or ideas — reach out at jadyenthelee@gmail.com.
