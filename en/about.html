About

What is Weavo

Weavo is a collaborative art project. Anyone can submit a piece of art and watch it become part of something larger — every submission is placed into a shared weavo based on its dominant color, building a picture that only comes together because everyone contributed a piece.

Why we built this

The project centers neurodivergent and neurotypical artists working side by side, with no separation between the two. Every piece counts equally toward the whole, regardless of who made it or how.

How it works

  1. Upload a piece of art from your profile — there's no project to pick.
  2. Weavo reads its dominant color automatically — no tagging required.
  3. It's matched into an open cell wherever that color fits best, across every project running at once. Nobody places pieces by hand, and no one chooses which project a piece joins.
  4. The picture builds itself one submission at a time, and only resolves into a whole once enough pieces have landed.

It's a mosaic in reverse: a mosaicist starts with a picture in mind and cuts pieces to fit it. Weavo starts with nothing but color logic — the artwork comes first, from people who've never seen each other's work, and the image only emerges in hindsight. Step back and it reads like any mosaic. Step closer and each "tile" turns out to be someone's complete artwork.

Who's behind it

Weavo is an independent, community-built project. Questions, feedback, or ideas — reach out at jadyenthelee@gmail.com.
