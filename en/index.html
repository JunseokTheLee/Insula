How It Works

From a single upload to a place in the whole.

Every piece follows the same four steps on its way from your device into the shared weavo — no curation, no manual placement, no waiting for approval.

  1. 01

    Submit your artwork

    Upload a painting, drawing, digital piece, or photograph from your profile — any medium is welcome. Add a title and a short description if you'd like people to know more about it once it's found.

  2. 02

    Weavo reads its color

    The moment you submit, Weavo analyzes your piece's dominant color automatically. There's no tagging, no category picking, and nothing to fill out beyond the artwork itself.

  3. 03

    It's matched to a project

    You don't pick a project yourself — your piece is placed into whichever open cell, across every active weavo, its color fits best. Matching is automatic and impartial, and each piece belongs to one project at a time.

  4. 04

    The picture comes together

    As more artists contribute, the full image gradually emerges from thousands of individual choices. Every piece stays visible at full size and credited to its artist — permanently part of the whole, never flattened into a single pixel.

Beyond the Canvas

Two more ways to explore the community.

The shared weavo isn't the only thing being built here. Network maps who's paying attention to whom, and Collections let you keep the pieces that matter to you close at hand.

Network

A living map of the whole community.

Every save draws a line — from the person doing the saving to the artist they saved. Bigger circles have more connections. Click anyone to light up their local network, drag nodes to rearrange the map, and click again to visit a profile.

Explore the Network →
Collections

Curate the pieces that matter to you.

Saving an artwork adds it to your pool — starting a Collection turns a handful of those pieces into something shaped on purpose. Give it a title and a short description of what ties it together, add or remove pieces any time, and choose whether it stays private or anyone can browse it.
